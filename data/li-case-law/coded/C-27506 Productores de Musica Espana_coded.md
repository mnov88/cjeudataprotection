A1.4: This is a pre-GDPR case (2008) that does not concern Article 6(1)(f) GDPR. It is included because the Court performed a balancing of fundamental rights (intellectual property and effective judicial protection vs. privacy and data protection) that is directly relevant to the development of the legitimate interest doctrine. The balancing framework applied here — requiring a "fair balance" between fundamental rights and invoking proportionality — prefigures the structure later codified in Article 6(1)(f) GDPR.

A5.2: The Court held that EU directives do not require Member States to lay down an obligation to disclose personal data in civil proceedings to ensure effective copyright protection. However, Member States must, when transposing and implementing those directives, strike a fair balance between the fundamental rights at stake — intellectual property and effective judicial protection on one side, and privacy and data protection on the other — while respecting the principle of proportionality.
