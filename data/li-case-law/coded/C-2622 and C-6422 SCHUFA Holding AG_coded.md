A5.1: N
A5.2: The Court held that the interests of the credit sector cannot justify the retention of personal data on discharge from remaining debts by a private credit information agency beyond the six-month period for which the data are kept in the public insolvency register. A code of conduct providing for a three-year retention period cannot override the conditions for lawfulness under Article 6(1)(f) GDPR. For the parallel storage period (while data are also in the public register), the Court left it to the referring court to determine whether such storage is strictly necessary.
