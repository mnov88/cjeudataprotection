A5.1: unclear
A5.2: The Court held that Article 7(f) of Directive 95/46 sets out two cumulative conditions for lawful processing based on legitimate interest -- necessity of the processing and non-override by the data subject's fundamental rights -- and that Member States cannot add additional requirements such as requiring data to appear in public sources. The Court emphasized that the balancing of interests must be carried out on a case-by-case basis, taking into account Articles 7 and 8 of the Charter, and that Member States may not categorically and in a generalised manner exclude processing of certain categories of personal data without allowing individual balancing.
