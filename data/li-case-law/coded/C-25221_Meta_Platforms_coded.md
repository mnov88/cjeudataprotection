A5.1: N
A5.2: The Court held that processing under Article 6(1)(f) GDPR can only be regarded as lawful on condition that the operator has informed users of the legitimate interest pursued, that processing is carried out only in so far as is strictly necessary for that interest, and that a balancing of opposing interests shows that users' interests and fundamental rights do not override the controller's interest. In the case of personalised advertising, the Court found that users of a free social network cannot reasonably expect such processing without consent, and the interests of the data subject override those of the controller. For network security, the interest was found legitimate in principle but the necessity of processing off-Facebook data remained to be verified by the referring court.
