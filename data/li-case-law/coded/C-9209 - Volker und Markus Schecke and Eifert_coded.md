A5.2: The Court held that the Council and Commission failed to properly balance the objective of transparency against the fundamental rights of natural persons under Articles 7 and 8 of the Charter. The blanket publication of names and exact amounts of all natural person beneficiaries, without any distinction based on duration, frequency, nature, or amount of aid, exceeded the limits imposed by the principle of proportionality and the strict necessity standard. For legal persons, however, the publication obligation was found proportionate.
