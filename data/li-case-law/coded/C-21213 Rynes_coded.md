A5.2: The Court did not rule on whether legitimate interest was a valid legal basis in this case. The case focused on whether the household exception in Article 3(2) of Directive 95/46 applied to home CCTV monitoring public space, which the Court answered in the negative. The Court merely noted in passing (paragraph 34) that the application of the Directive makes it possible to take into account legitimate interests pursued by the controller, such as protection of property, health and life, under Articles 7(f), 11(2), and 13(1)(d) and (g).
